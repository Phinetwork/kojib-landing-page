Cookie Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2024

1. Introduction

Kojib is committed to transparency in how we collect, use, and manage cookies on our website. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and your options regarding their use.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to remember your preferences and actions over time to provide a more personalized experience.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for the functionality of our website and services. These cannot be disabled.
  • Performance Cookies: Help us analyze website usage to improve functionality and user experience.
  • Functionality Cookies: Store user preferences and settings for a more tailored experience.
  • Advertising Cookies: Used to deliver relevant ads and track campaign performance.

4. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience on our website, including:

  • Remembering your login details and preferences.
  • Analyzing website traffic and performance.
  • Delivering personalized content and advertisements.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect your ability to use certain features of our website. For more information, consult your browser's help section.

6. Third-Party Cookies

Kojib may partner with third-party services that use cookies to provide analytics, advertising, or other functionalities. We do not have control over these third-party cookies. Please review their respective privacy and cookie policies for details.

7. Updates to This Cookie Policy

Kojib reserves the right to update this Cookie Policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page, and the "Effective Date" will be updated accordingly.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Cookie Policy, please contact us at contact@kojib.com.
