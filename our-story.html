Kojib’s story doesn’t start in a boardroom or a corporate lab—it begins with a simple idea: that technology could do more than solve problems. It could inspire. It could connect. It could transform the way we live and dream. In a small space, filled with notebooks, circuit boards, and boundless ambition, the founders set out to create something timeless.
