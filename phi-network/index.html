What is Phi Network?

Phi Network is a fully decentralized AI-driven blockchain ecosystem designed to create a trustless, verifiable, and transparent knowledge economy.

By combining artificial intelligence, blockchain, and economic incentives, Phi Network introduces a new paradigm of AI decentralization, transparency, and reward-driven validation.

At the center of it all is Asterion – an AI whose knowledge is validated on-chain through a unique Proof-of-Inference (PoI) consensus mechanism.

How Phi Network Works

Phi Network is powered by Proof-of-Inference (PoI), where AI outputs are verified by a network of validators staking Phi Tokens.

1

AI Generates Insights

Asterion produces predictions, creative content, or data-driven analysis, ready for on-chain verification.

2

Validators Stake & Verify

Users stake Phi Tokens and vote on correctness. Rewards are distributed for accurate validation.

3

On-Chain Knowledge

Validated knowledge is stored as immutable records, forming a transparent and trustless AI knowledge base.
